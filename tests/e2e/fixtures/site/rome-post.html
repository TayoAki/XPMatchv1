Three slow days in Rome

We flew in on a Thursday and checked into Hotel Artemide on Via Nazionale. The rooftop hot tub at sunset was the reason we booked it, and it did not disappoint, though the street below is busy until late.

Where we ate

Roscioli Salumeria con Cucina is the carbonara everyone talks about, and it is worth the fuss if you book ahead. Trattoria Da Enzo al 29 in Trastevere does the cacio e pepe we still think about; go at opening or queue.

A friend swears by a tiny place called Il Posto Segreto near the river, but we never found it and cannot say where exactly it is.

A green afternoon

Villa Borghese gave us the break we needed between churches: rent a bike, then walk down to Piazza del Popolo.

  • Book Roscioli at least a week out.
  • Villa Borghese is free; the gallery inside is not.

Written by a traveler who prefers slow mornings and long lunches.
