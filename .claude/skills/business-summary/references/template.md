# Business Summary — document template

Follow this order exactly. Section names are fixed so Compare mode can parse them. Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Delete a module section only if its business type does not apply; never delete a core section.

The source listings (Quiet Light Brokerage packages, 2023–2026) use this same spine: Approval, Disclosure, Process, Executive Summary with Financial Quickview, Financial Overview, Traffic Overview, Client Interview (numbered Q&A by section), Clear Path to Growth, SWOT, Contact. Three sections are added for a business that does not exist yet: Key Risks, Launch Plan & Financing, and the Validation Scorecard.

## 2. Disclosure

This document describes a business that has not yet launched. It is a planning and decision document, not an offer to sell securities or a solicitation of investment.

Forward-looking statements: the projections here are built from the assumptions listed in `2-financial-model.md` and are subject to risks the founders do not control. Projections tagged Assumed have no supporting evidence yet; the Validation Scorecard in Section 8 lists the tests that would change that.

Competitor information was gathered from public sources on the dates cited. It may be incomplete or out of date.

## 3. From Summary to Launch

How this document is meant to be used:

1. **Review** — every founder and partner reads this summary and the financial model in full.
2. **Challenge** — each reader writes down the three numbers they believe least. Those go to the Validation Scorecard if they are not already there.
3. **Validate** — run the Scorecard tests in cost order. Each test either moves a number to Verified or kills the idea. Budget: [$X] and [N] weeks.
4. **Decide** — go / no-go on the stated capital and hours. A "go" means the numbers in the Quickview are the commitment.
5. **Build** — [what gets built first, by whom, in how long].
6. **Launch** — [the first channel and the first offer].
7. **Milestone review** — at [90 days / first $X revenue], re-run `/business-summary` in Update mode and compare actuals to the Year-1 monthly plan.
